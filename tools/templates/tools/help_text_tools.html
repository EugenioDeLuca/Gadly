Tips for preparing your text

Quick suggestions for using the Word & Character Counter and other text tools.

Counting words correctly

Word count is based on spaces: multiple spaces or line breaks are treated as separators between words.

Before publishing

  • Check both word and character count when you have limits (for example, social posts).
  • Remove extra spaces or line breaks to avoid inflating the count.
